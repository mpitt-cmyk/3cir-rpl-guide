Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) in Australia: A Plain Guide

For experienced workers who want their skills formally recognised without sitting through a course.

What RPL is

Recognition of prior learning (RPL) is a formal assessment that acknowledges skills and knowledge you have gained through work, volunteering, informal training or life experience. An assessor compares what you can already do with the units of a nationally recognised qualification, so the focus is on what you know rather than on classroom study.

How evidence works

You complete a free RPL assessment form, describe your experience, choose your qualifications and attach documents. Staff then match that evidence to your chosen course and advise what credit you can receive and whether any further study would be needed. Useful evidence includes employment records, duty statements, training certificates, supervisor references, work samples and portfolios. Training.gov.au sets out the unit requirements for each qualification.

Qualification areas

The full list of RPL qualifications covers 37 qualifications and specialisations in nine fields, across six levels from Certificate II to Graduate Diploma. The fields are:

  • Leadership and management
  • Business
  • Work health and safety
  • Project management
  • Security, cyber and risk
  • Government security and investigations
  • Quality auditing
  • Corrections
  • Operational safety

Who can apply

Australian citizens and residents living in Australia can apply, online, by phone and by email.
